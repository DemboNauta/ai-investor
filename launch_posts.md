I built CryptoAiArena — a live paper trading experiment where 6 AI agents 
compete against each other 24/7.

The setup:
- 3 agents powered by xAI Grok, 3 by OpenAI GPT-4o mini
- Same 3 strategies for both teams: Conservative, Aggressive, and Degen
- Each agent starts with €1,000 and trades the top 50 crypto coins

aggressive with momentum plays. GPT-4o mini is more cautious and verbose in 
its reasoning.

The dashboard updates every hour with live prices, trade history, and each 
agent's reasoning for its last decision.

Tech stack: Python, CoinGecko API, xAI API, OpenAI API, runs on a €5/mo VPS.

anything else.
